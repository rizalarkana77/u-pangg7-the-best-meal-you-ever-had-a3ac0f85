## Entry

The best meal I ever had was my grandmother's rendang, eaten with my hands on her kitchen floor while rain hammered the zinc roof and she pretended not to notice my third helping.

## Why this sentence

This sentence wins because every detail is concrete and sensory — hands instead of a spoon, the kitchen floor instead of a table, rain hammering a zinc roof — so you can see, hear, and almost taste the scene instead of reading an abstract claim about a good meal. It is personal in the way only a real memory is: a grandmother who loves you enough to pretend not to notice a third helping says more about that meal than any adjective about flavor ever could. And it stands completely alone as one sentence, carrying place, weather, food, and love in a single breath, which is exactly what this bounty asks for.

## Verification

Run with Node (stdlib only):

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output node main.ts
cat output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, validates the bounty gate (single sentence, at most 200 characters), then reads the file back and verifies it byte-for-byte before logging the result.
